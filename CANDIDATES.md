# The 3 AM Excuse — candidates

Gate: `/output/entry.txt`, UTF-8, 1–200 characters, one line, not
whitespace only. Judge (in order): (1) on subject — an excuse a
hardware wallet gives for refusing to sign a transaction at 3 AM;
(2) original angle — NOT batteries, firmware updates or cold storage;
(3) the wallet's own voice, a device with its own priorities and
grudges; (4) the 3 AM hour itself is the joke — the line could not be
pasted onto a 3 PM transaction; (5) tight, every word earning its
place, no explaining the joke.

## Picked: #1

Why it wins against the judge brief, criterion by criterion:

1. On subject: unmistakably a hardware wallet refusing to sign, at
   3 AM, in the wallet's own words.
2. Original: the angle is the wallet's own memory of the owner's
   regret pattern — nobody else's excuse is a longitudinal study of
   its owner's bad decisions. No battery, firmware or cold-storage
   joke anywhere.
3. Wallet's voice: "in my memory" is literally device language, and
   "I'm breaking the cycle" is a device with a grudge and a policy.
4. The hour IS the joke: a 3 AM signature that is regretted by
   9 AM. Paste this onto 3 PM and the whole line collapses.
5. Economy: 118 characters, three beats (the pattern, the refusal,
   the punchline "ask the sun, not me"), nothing explained.

Runner-up: #11 (the "alibi" angle) — good voice, but "morning self
will deny" is vaguer than #1's concrete 3 AM → 9 AM regret loop.
#2 is strong too, but it states the hour instead of turning the hour
into the joke.
